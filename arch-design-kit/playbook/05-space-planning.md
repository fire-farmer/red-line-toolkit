# 05 · Space planning

Code minimums keep people safe. They don't make rooms that work. This chapter gives the **design targets** the playbook plans to by default (SP-SIZE-1), plus the kitchen, bath, circulation, storage and furniture rules that make a plan livable.

Targets are *Thumb* or *Practice* rules. Go below a target only with a reason in `DECISIONS.md`. Never go below the code floor in chapter 02.

## SP-SIZE-1: room size targets

Sizes are net, inside finished walls. "Compact" is the smallest size that still works well. "Comfortable" is the default.

| Room | Code floor | Compact | Comfortable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary bedroom | 70 sf, 7 ft | 10×11 ft | 11×12 ft | Fits a queen bed with 24 in. or more on both sides and the foot; closet extra |
| Primary bedroom | 70 sf, 7 ft | 12×13 ft | 14×16 ft | King bed with 36 in. on both sides; consider a sitting area |
| Living room | 70 sf, 7 ft | 12×14 ft | 15×18 ft | Conversation circle ≤ 10 ft across; TV wall and fireplace don't fight the windows |
| Dining (6 seats) | 70 sf, 7 ft | 10×11 ft | 12×13 ft | 36 in. table edge to wall; 44 in. where people walk behind chairs |
| Kitchen | (exempt) | 10×12 ft | 12×16 ft with island | See SP-KIT |
| Full bath | — | 5×8 ft | 8×10 ft | Tub or shower plus toilet and lavatory; see SP-BATH |
| Powder room | — | 3×6 ft | 4×6 ft | Door must not swing into the toilet's clear space |
| Laundry | 6′-8″ ceiling | 6×6 ft | 7×9 ft | Exterior wall for the dryer vent (M-DRYER-1) |
| Entry / foyer | — | 6×6 ft | 8×8 ft | Coat closet adjacent (SP-STOR-1) |
| Hall | 36 in. | 42 in. | 48 in. | 48 in. or more where it is also a gallery or universal design is on |
| Stair width | 36 in. | 40 in. | 42–48 in. | Wider stairs move furniture more easily |
| Two-car garage | — | 22×22 ft | 24×24 ft | Add 2–4 ft of depth for storage or a workbench (SP-GAR-1) |
| Walk-in closet | — | 5×7 ft | 7×8 ft | 24 in. hanging depth + 36 in. aisle per side |

These are rules of thumb from residential practice, not a sourced standard. Calibrate them to the client and the budget.

## SP-KIT-1 / SP-KIT-2: kitchens (NKBA)

The NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines are recommendations, not code (⚠️ the values below are from the widely cited 2012 edition and are queued for verification).

**Clearances**
- Work aisle ≥ **42 in.** for one cook and ≥ **48 in.** for two or more (SP-KIT-1, auto). Walkway ≥ **36 in.**
- Seating: **32 in.** from a counter or table edge to an obstruction if no one passes; **36 in.** to edge past a seated person; **44 in.** to walk past.
- Knee space per seat, 24 in. wide: 18 in. deep at a 30 in. counter, 15 in. at 36 in., 12 in. at 42 in.

**Work triangle and landing areas** (SP-KIT-2)
- Sink, cooktop and refrigerator: the three legs sum to ≤ **26 ft**, each leg is **4–9 ft**, and no leg crosses an island by more than 12 in. No through-traffic in the triangle.
- Sink landing ≥ **24 in.** on one side and ≥ **18 in.** on the other. Prep area ≥ **36×24 in.** next to a sink.
- Cooktop ≥ **12 in.** on one side and **15 in.** on the other. At an island, 9 in. behind it.
- Refrigerator **15 in.** on the handle side. Oven and microwave **15 in.** adjacent or within 48 in. across.
- Dishwasher within **36 in.** of the sink edge, with 21 in. of standing space beside it.
- Total countertop frontage ≥ **158 in.**, 24 in. deep, with 15 in. clear above.

**Layout defaults:** an L or U plus an island. Pantry and mudroom adjacent (P-ZONE-1 service spine). The range not under a window. A ducted hood to outdoors (M-VENT-1).

## SP-BATH-1: baths (NKBA targets over code)

| Item | Code floor (C-BATH) | NKBA target |
|---|---|---|
| Toilet centerline to side wall/fixture | 15 in. | **18 in.** |
| Clear in front of toilet | 21 in. | **30 in.** (48 in. ideal) |
| Clear floor at each fixture | — | **30×48 in.** |
| Shower interior | 900 sq in., 30 in. circle | **36×36 in.** or larger |
| Toilet compartment | — (IRC) | **36×66 in.** |
| Lavatory centerline to side wall | 15 in. | **20 in.**; 36 in. between double-bowl centerlines |
| Door | — | ≥ 32 in. clear; swings in only if the clear floor stays usable, otherwise a pocket door |

- Never put a toilet on axis with the bathroom door (P-PRIV-1).
- Put blocking for grab bars at the toilet, tub and shower in every bath. It costs almost nothing during framing (SP-UD-3).

## SP-CIRC-1: circulation

- Circulation (halls, foyer, stair, landings, mudroom passage) is a cost: it gets built, heated and cleaned. Aim for **about 10–15%** of net area. The checker reports the figure and warns above **20%** (auto).
- No hall should serve only one door. Shorten it, fold it into a room, or make it work as a gallery, library or storage wall.
- Circulation through a room should run along one edge, not cut it in half diagonally. Mark the paths in the furniture plan (SP-FURN-1).

## SP-STOR-1: storage

- A coat closet at the front entry and a drop zone at the garage/mudroom entry, with a bench, hooks and lockers.
- Linen storage near the bedroom baths.
- A closet in every bedroom, at least **24 in.** deep. Secondary bedrooms get about 5–6 ft of rod; the primary gets a walk-in (SP-SIZE-1).
- A pantry next to the kitchen: walk-in or a 24 in. deep cabinet wall.
- Mechanical, water heater, panel and bulk storage planned on purpose (M-CORE-1, M-ELEC-1), not left to leftover space.

## SP-FURN-1: furnish every room

A room isn't designed until its furniture fits with clearances:

- Beds: 24 in. minimum and 36 in. preferred at the sides and foot. Nightstands about 24 in. wide.
- Dining: chairs need 36 in. from the table edge to a wall, and 44 in. where people walk behind them.
- Living: seating within a **10 ft** conversation circle. TV distance about 1.5–2.5× the screen diagonal (⚠️ rule of thumb).
- Every room has at least one uninterrupted wall for the main furniture piece: bed headboard, sofa or dining buffet. Doors, windows and vents should not use up all four walls.

## SP-DOOR-1: doors

- Doors swing **into rooms, never into halls**, and never into each other or against a fixture.
- A door opening lands against a wall, not in the middle of a room. Hinge it so it folds back against the nearest wall.
- Where swings collide or space is tight, use pocket or barn doors. Code requires a side-hinged door at the egress door only (C-DOOR-1).
- Standard interior doors: 30–32 in. for baths and closets, 32–36 in. for bedrooms, **36 in. everywhere with universal design on** (SP-UD-1).

## SP-UD: universal design and accessibility tiers

Accessibility in private homes is a **per-project tier** chosen in the brief (SP-UD-3). Values are drawn from ADA 2010 and ICC A117.1 as targets (⚠️ not IRC requirements).

| Tier | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Code only |
| **Visitable** | One zero-step entrance (thresholds ≤ ½ in.); 32 in. clear doors on the entry level; an entry-level half bath with 30×48 in. clear floor |
| **Aging in place / universal** | Visitable, plus: an entry-level primary suite; **36 in.** doors (about 34 in. clear, SP-UD-1, auto); a **60 in.** turning circle in that bath (SP-UD-2, auto); a curbless shower ≥ 60×30 in. (or 36×36 transfer); 36 in. routes; switches and outlets at 15–48 in.; grab-bar blocking everywhere; ramps ≤ 1:12 |

## SP-GAR-1: garages

- Two-car: **22×22 ft** minimum, **24×24 ft** comfortable. One-car: 12×22 ft minimum. Add depth for storage.
- Garage door: 16×7 ft (double) or 9×7 ft (single). Use an 8 ft height for trucks or roof racks.
- The route from the garage to the kitchen passes a drop zone (mudroom). Keep at least one step or a ramp at the house door per the AHJ. Never open the garage into a bedroom (C-GAR-2).
- Put the garage on the north or west to buffer the house (P-ORIENT-1). Keep it from dominating the street façade unless the style or neighborhood calls for it.
