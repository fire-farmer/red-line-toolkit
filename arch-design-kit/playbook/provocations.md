# Provocations and analogies: the concept deck

`tools/deal.py` deals these cards to the concept lenses (CR-SPECIFIC-1). Each lens gets different cards, chosen by a seed from the project name, so no two lenses (and no two projects) start from the same anchors. Prompts should **never** list examples from this page directly. A list of examples in a prompt becomes the answer. Add cards freely: one per line, under the right heading.

## Analogies

- a monastery cloister: life arranged around a quiet walk
- a boat cabin: every inch built in, nothing loose
- a Japanese engawa: the veranda strip between house and garden
- a barn's threshing floor: one big central space, side bays for the rest
- a library stack: walls that are storage, rooms carved out of them
- a train: rooms in a line, the corridor along one side, a view out of every room
- a campsite: separate tents around a fire
- a lighthouse: one tall room, a long view, a stair as the spine
- a market hall: a big roof over a scatter of smaller things
- a tree house: rooms perched among existing trunks
- a walled kitchen garden: a sheltered outdoor room that warms early in spring
- a ship's bridge: one room that sees in every direction
- a cave: thick, dark and cool, with one bright mouth
- a covered bridge: a passage that is also a room
- a tent: a light roof on a few poles, walls that open
- a Roman atrium: a hole in the roof, rain falling into the center
- a sleeping porch: rooms that are half outside
- a theater: a stage, an audience, and the backstage
- a hearth hall: everything gathered around one fire
- an orangery: a glass room for warmth in winter
- a dogtrot: two cabins under one roof, a breezeway between them
- a pavilion in a park: a roof, a view, and almost no walls
- a cabinet of curiosities: small rooms of collected things
- a terraced vineyard: floors stepping down a slope, each with its own view
- a farmyard: buildings arranged around a working court
- a harbor wall: a heavy back against the weather, open to the water
- a pergola walk: shade and dappled light along a path
- a bathhouse: a sequence from cool to warm, dressed to undressed
- a schoolroom: big windows on one side, everyone faces the light
- a bell tower: the vertical as the event

## Provocations

- the largest room is outdoors
- no corridor anywhere
- one material, inside and out
- design the stair first
- the roof is the only façade
- every room has two doors
- every room touches the garden
- the kitchen is the center of the plan, literally
- no room is rectangular except the bedrooms
- the house is one room with alcoves
- half the house is a porch
- a wall you live inside: 4 ft thick, full of storage and seats
- the bedrooms are small cabins; the rest is shared
- the entry is through the garden, never through a hall
- light enters only from above in the living spaces
- every window frames one specific thing
- the house steps with the ground; no flat site allowed
- the garage is a garden room the rest of the year
- one big roof, everything under it
- the plan works without any interior doors except baths
- the building is two buildings
- the section does the work: one double-height space connects everything
- design it for the hottest day first
- the house is narrower than 16 ft
- a room that is only for one ritual
- nothing faces the street except the door
- every room has a place to sit by a window
- the dining table is the biggest piece of architecture
- the bathroom is the best room in the house
- the house turns as you go through it: no two rooms face the same way
